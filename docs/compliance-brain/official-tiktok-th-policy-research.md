## Fingerprint basis

`NORMALIZED_TEXT_V1` means SHA-256 of the retrieved article text snapshot, **not raw publisher HTML**. The snapshot starts at the article heading and ends before its helpfulness footer. The browser's `L<number>:` line annotations and citation decorations are removed; link labels, headings, displayed dates and image placeholders remain. Normalization uses NFC, LF line endings, trims/collapses horizontal whitespace per line, collapses runs of three or more newlines, and trims the result. Source ranges are recorded only to describe this research snapshot; future retrievals must fingerprint their own extracted text.

The JSON records retrieval at date precision (`retrievedDate`) and the metadata recording timestamp separately (`snapshotRecordedAt`). It does not invent an exact HTTP retrieval timestamp. Dynamic localization or revised article text can produce a changed hash; that change starts a new candidate and requires review.

## Activation boundary

These paraphrases are research proposals, not definitive legal classification or production authorization. `PolicyRegistry` verifies official URLs, source-text hashes, approved candidate equality, scope, effective dates, semantic categories, regression results and administrator authority. Packs are Ed25519 signed and immutable by ID/version. The production database and trusted public keys are server owned. Unavailable or unsigned policies produce review-required behavior rather than a safe result.
