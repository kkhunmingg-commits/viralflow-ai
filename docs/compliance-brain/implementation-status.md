# Compliance Brain implementation status

Local implementation review: 8 October 2026. This document records repository behavior and local verification; it does not certify a deployed environment or authorize policy activation.

## Implemented locally

- Owner-scoped product evidence, permitted claims, final-media attestations and immutable POST/LIVE decisions have a SQL foundation. Customer roles can read their own ledger and aggregate decision inputs; they cannot manufacture verdicts, verify claims, write media attestations or activate policy packs.
- Decision recording writes the verdict, audit and initial feedback in one transaction, rejects altered retries, and holds a safe verdict for review when its audit cannot be recorded. Final-media attestations bind verified `MEDIA_REVIEW` evidence to the actual asset hash.
- Feedback and learning tables remain private. After a decision and its audit are persisted, the database runtime attempts a bounded aggregate refresh for that policy version. Optional learning failure is logged and does not change the audited verdict. The internal learning endpoint also accepts administrator-entered human corrections or evidence-linked platform outcomes and supports explicit refresh. All refreshes use a single bounded database snapshot. Global patterns exclude customer identifiers, scripts, comments, sales, free-text reasons and evidence links. Fewer than five contributing tenants suppresses global persistence. Automatic refresh cannot approve or enforce a candidate, overwrite a newer snapshot, or remove an existing verified/enforced approval. Candidate shadow evaluation replays typed historical signals and reports evaluated versus unavailable counts separately from hypothetical block/rewrite counts. It does not mirror stored verdict labels, run an independent holdout evaluation or establish calibrated confidence. Training evidence and shadow replay populations are counted separately. The verdict engine does not consume learned candidates as policy rules.
- The internal console and admin API authenticate with `getUser()` and require the server-controlled `app_metadata.viralflow_role` value `admin` or `developer`. Activation verifies the signed envelope with server trusted public keys before calling the service-only transactional RPC. Version identity and reviewed payloads are immutable; reviewed versions cannot return to a draft state to evade that guard.
- Customer Account Safety uses persisted final checks from the last 30 days, counts the latest check per content hash and channel, and distinguishes missing data from unavailable storage. The endpoint checks account ownership before reading results. The customer card displays aggregate counts and Thai status text, inherits the app theme and excludes internal identifiers, policy versions, reasons and rewrite traces from its serialized summary and rendered markup. A customer-safe derived boolean distinguishes a rewrite that was applied and passed from an unapplied rewrite proposal, which remains review-required.
- AUTO's `COMPLIANCE_CHECK` reruns the current engine even when an older eligibility result exists. The final POST gate inspects the exact immutable Blob later uploaded, including the approved caption/disclosure. Claim and submission snapshots must retain the checked account, video, source, settings and consent. External `PULL_FROM_URL` delivery stays held because an immutable binding to the provider's fetched bytes is not established.

## Readiness work still required

1. **Official policy approval and provisioning.** The researched Thailand candidate in `tiktok-th-research-candidate.json` is `PARSED / HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING`, unsigned and inactive. It is not a production policy pack. Re-fetch and review source snapshots, exceptions, applicability, graphics and the documented date discrepancy. Every researched effective date remains `UNKNOWN`; publication dates must not become invented commencement dates. Complete administrator review, meaningful regression validation, Ed25519 signing and persistence before activation. The console currently manages persisted versions; it does not provide an import, review or signing workflow.
2. **Server authority and database setup.** Apply and verify the migration through the approved deployment process, provision the server-controlled administrator role and trusted public keys, and protect the signer outside customer-facing code. No production credentials, trusted keys, roles or policy records were provisioned by this local review. The database runtime uses an in-process last-known-good envelope only while its lease is less than 60 seconds old, measured from the successful lookup's start. Slow reads that exhaust the lease, backwards clock movement during a read or against a cache stamp, expired leases and untrusted signatures do not extend cached authority. A fresh lookup with no active pack clears fallback immediately. During an outage, another instance's retirement can remain unseen within that remaining lease; this is a bounded acceptance window, not immediate distributed revocation. Keys and emergency retirement must be propagated to every runtime. Restart loses this cache and requires review if the database remains unavailable. The separate file registry preserves durable local envelopes but does not provide durable fallback for the database runtime.
3. **Verified product claims.** Populate owner/product-scoped evidence and exact approved claims, channels, conditions, jurisdiction and expiry through a controlled server process. No customer verification or claim-provisioning UI/API is supplied here. An ordinary product description does not automatically become verified evidence or authority to make a claim.
4. **Actual-media review.** The default media scanner checks an existing verified attestation and complete coverage; it does not analyze video frames, perform OCR or transcribe audio. A trusted human review or validated vision/transcription pipeline must inspect the actual finished asset and persist its observations with matching evidence and hash. Script text, a generated filename or a stored checksum alone cannot establish visual compliance. Missing, expired, incomplete or mismatched review stays held for review.
5. **Policy and learning operations.** Official-source ingestion and diff helpers are available, but no autonomous policy watcher, recurring source retrieval, production candidate importer or signing service is provisioned. Platform outcomes are administrator-entered observations linked to an official HTTPS reference; they are not fetched or independently verified through a platform API. The learning refresh reads at most 5,000 feedback rows and rejects an incomplete snapshot before writing patterns; a larger policy history remains pending and needs a separately validated collection strategy. Learning thresholds and lifecycle helpers are a foundation for separately validated administrator approval. No approval/enforcement workflow is connected to the verdict engine, and candidates never alter a verdict or activate a signed policy pack automatically.
6. **Operational validation.** The default semantic classifier combines contextual risk patterns with strict grounding against exact verified claims and approved aliases. Outgoing questions, including rhetorical questions without a recognized risk phrase, still require grounding; missing support stays held or requires an applied rewrite that passes a new check. This is bounded classification and claim validation, not full open-ended semantic understanding. The optional local semantic model requires separate local model provisioning and evaluation and cannot override the grounding layer. Local LIVE decisions use durable private local audits before speech release; no cloud ingestion of these local audits is supplied, so cloud Account Safety does not automatically include all local LIVE checks. Verify the complete POST/LIVE path, latency, disclosure, evidence expiry, source updates, revoked trust, feedback ingestion and administrator rollback in the target environment. A synthetic signed fixture or passing local test is not official policy authority or a platform acceptance guarantee.
